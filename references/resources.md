## Verification Notes

1. **Alahing Sasih folio citation.** The specific claim that the formula "*wewaran alah dening wuku…*" sits on folio 11a of Wariga Gemet traces to Ida Pedanda Gede Buruan's *Padewasan Kapelek* (via Tribun Bali). **This has not been independently verified against the digitized folios.** Verifying this requires reading the scanned manuscripts in Section 1 and cross-checking against related lontar in Section 3.

2. **Multiple manuscript recensions.** "Wariga Gemet" and "Wariga Gede Gemet" are likely different copies — possibly with textual variation. Treat single-manuscript claims as provisional until cross-validated.

3. **Tri Wara market-day claim.** The `rgas pasar` ↔ Tri Wara mapping is documented in the Historia.id article citing Bali Kuno epigraphy (Section 5). The specific "3-village rotating circuit" model is **not** directly attested and should be treated as hypothesis until confirmed via the Leiden KITLV or Indonesian academic sources.

4. **Licensing.** Wikimedia Commons files are PD Indonesia. Internet Archive lontar scans appear to be public domain by age. Scribd copies should be treated as unverified mirrors. Academic papers vary; the listed open-access links are confirmed accessible as of April 2026.

5. **Quality tier priority for research.** Primary lontar scans (§1) > peer-reviewed academic (§4) > historical documentation (§5) > practitioner interpretation (§8). Use practitioner sources for living practice context, not for historical claims.
